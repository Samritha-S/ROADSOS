## Slide 2 – The Problem
- ~170 000 road deaths in India per year (≈ 1 every 3 min).
- Most incidents occur on highways with poor/​no cellular signal.
- Victims often unconscious; by‑standers don’t know how to summon help.
- Existing 108 service fails when there is no signal or awareness.

---
## Slide 3 – Our Solution
- **RoadSOS**: an offline‑first, AI‑enabled emergency assistant.
- Works without network using a bundled 5 MB SQLite contact DB (hospitals, police, ambulance).
- One‑tap SOS launches triage, location capture, and auto‑dial/​SMS to nearest responder.
- Built with Flutter for cross‑platform reach (Android & iOS).

---
## Slide 4 – Stand‑out Features
1. **Multilingual** – 8 Indian + 3 European languages (Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati, Kannada, Spanish, French, German).
2. **Extensible Plugin System** – Add custom alerts (e.g., SMS to a user‑defined contact).
3. **Battery Optimisation** – Low‑power GPS, background‑friendly isolates.
4. **AI‑Powered Triage** – Gemini‑4 model suggests immediate first‑aid steps.

---
## Slide 5 – Future Scope
- Expand to 20+ languages & add voice‑based SOS.
- Integrate with state EMS APIs for real‑time dispatch.
- Deploy edge‑AI models for offline injury severity prediction.
- Open‑source SDK for NGOs to embed RoadSOS in community apps.
